# Multistage Stochastic Optimization Projects

This repository contains a collection of multistage stochastic optimization models developed for strategic and operational planning in agriculture. These models are designed to support decision-making under uncertainty and address key areas such as:

- **Harvest Scheduling & Machinery Allocation (Mahdavimanshadi M, Yao S, Fan N. Optimal guayule harvest planning and machinery scheduling under drought scenarios in semi-arid farms. Smart Agricultural Technology. 2024 Mar 1;7:100420.)** 
- **Precision Irrigation Planning**
- **Crop Rotation Optimization (Mahdavimanshadi M, Fan N. Multistage Stochastic Optimization for Semi-arid Farm Crop Rotation and Water Irrigation Scheduling Under Drought Scenarios. Journal of Agricultural, Biological and Environmental Statistics. 2024 Sep 5:1-24.)**

The focus is on enhancing sustainability and efficiency in agricultural operations, especially under uncertain conditions like weather variability and water availability.
